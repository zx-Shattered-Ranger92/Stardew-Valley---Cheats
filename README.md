## What it does

Stardew Valley is a farming sim about building a life from scratch — clearing land, planting crops, mining ore, fishing, making friends, and restoring the Community Center. The game gives you fifteen minutes per day and asks you to choose what matters most. The early game is famously slow: you run out of energy by 9am, your watering can holds next to nothing, and the first season is a slog through fatigue and limited gold.

A cheat menu for Stardew is not about skipping the farm — it is about *rescuing* the days you wish you had back. The `Infinite Energy` and `Time of Day` options are the real value here: they let you actually finish what you started before passing out at 2am. `Unlimited Items` and `Auto Water` remove the busywork, not the planning. This is the difference between feeling *chore* and feeling *choice*.

## Hotkeys

`Insert` opens the menu · `End` resets everything · `F1`–`F12` as above, all rebindable · arrow keys and `Enter` navigate without a mouse

> [!TIP]
> Enable `Auto Water Crops` and `Auto Pet Animals` — this saves thirty in-game minutes every morning without removing the satisfaction of watching your farm grow. Set `Time of Day` to freeze at 6am and you can clear the entire farm before breakfast. For fishing, `Instant Catch` + `Perfect Catch` turns the minigame into a treasure collector. The game's charm stays — you just skip the parts that make you close the game.

> [!WARNING]
> `Crop Growth Speed` at max (`100x`) means crops grow in minutes. It collapses the game's entire economy and removes the planning that makes farming satisfying. `Instant Kill` in the mines skips the combat progression entirely. Both are fun for a second save, but they will empty a first playthrough fast. Use them deliberately, not by default.
>
> Options tagged `save` write persistent data that a patch can invalidate. Back up first and disable cloud sync while you experiment.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>Will I get banned?</summary>
No. Single-player only, no anti-cheat, no ranked mode. Achievements unlock locally unless you block them in the menu.
</details>

<details>
<summary>My energy runs out too fast — what do I use?</summary>
`Infinite Energy` (`F1`) is the first thing most people turn on. Stardew's energy system is punishing in year one, and this lets you actually play the game instead of going to bed at 10am. Pair it with `Movement Speed` at `2x` and the early game becomes enjoyable rather than a trudge.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Fishing is impossible — can you fix it?</summary>
Yes. `Instant Catch` (`F7`) removes the minigame entirely — you cast and the fish is caught immediately. `Perfect Catch` (`F8`) adds treasure chests to every catch. This is the most common complaint about Stardew, and this solves it completely.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does it work with mods like SMAPI?</summary>
Yes, but load order matters. Launch Stardew Valley through SMAPI first, then attach this trainer. Some mods that change core gameplay (like Stardew Valley Expanded) may break specific options — test one at a time.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does it work on Steam Deck or Linux?</summary>
No. Windows only. Proton changes how the game's memory is laid out and this build does not handle that.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Windows Defender flagged the download.</summary>
Trainers read and write another process's memory, which is what a lot of malware also does, so heuristic scanners flag them on principle. Every release ships with a SHA256 checksum and full source. Add an exclusion if you are comfortable with that — and if you would rather not, don't. That is a reasonable call.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Options stopped working after an update.</summary>
Patches move memory offsets and options fail independently, so some will keep working. Check the Releases page for a build matching your game version. Stardew updates frequently; we try to keep up within 48 hours of major patches.
</details>

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| Nothing happens on `Insert` | Another overlay grabbed the key — Steam, Discord or RTSS. Rebind the menu key. |
| "Process not found" | The game must be running with a save loaded. Launch it first, then attach. |
| `Unlimited Items` does nothing | The option works on held stacks. Place the item in your inventory, select it, then toggle. It does not create items from nothing — it prevents depletion. |
| `Max Skills` did not save | The change is not permanent unless you save the game afterward. Sleep a day to write it to your save file. |
| `Warp` options teleported me to the wrong place | Warps work from any location, but some areas (like the Skull Cavern) have specific teleport restrictions. Use `Move to Exit` first if you are underground. |
| Unlocks vanished after a patch | A persistent write was invalidated. Restore a backup from before the update. |
